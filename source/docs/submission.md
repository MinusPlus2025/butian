## 一句话
天塌了，洪水不息。你是女娲：在洪水淹没山谷之前，按五行相生之序炼石补天，把重新有了颜色的世界送给第一批来到这里的人。

## One-liner
The sky has cracked and the flood will not stop. You are Nüwa: forge five elemental stones in the order of the generating cycle, mend the sky before the valley drowns, and give the restored world to the first people who arrive.

## 中文简介
《补天》取材于《淮南子·览冥训》："往古之时，四极废，九州裂，天不兼覆……水浩洋而不息。"
开场的山谷是一片褪色的灰，天上横着一道发光的裂缝，水位在不断上涨。玩家在山谷中寻找五行原石（木、火、土、金、水），带回铜炉炼成五色石，石头飞上天空补住一段裂缝，对应的那片土地随即恢复颜色。

核心规则来自五行相生：第一块由你自由选择，之后每一块必须由上一块所"生"（木生火、火生土、土生金、金生水、水生木）。顺序错了，石头会碎，洪水猛涨。低处的原石最先被淹，所以从哪一块开始、走哪条路，是每一局真正要做的决定。

五块补齐，天合拢，水退去，第一批人走进山谷。这个世界是女娲送给他们的礼物。

## English description
Mending the Sky is a short playable myth based on the Chinese legend of Nüwa, recorded in the Huainanzi (2nd century BC): "The four pillars broke, the nine provinces split, the sky no longer covered the earth… the waters flooded without end."

You start in a valley drained of colour under a glowing crack in the sky, with the flood rising. Find the five elemental ores (wood, fire, earth, metal, water), carry each one to the bronze furnace and forge it into a coloured stone. The stone flies up and seals part of the crack, and the land beneath it gets its colour back.

The rule comes from the Wuxing generating cycle. Your first stone is free; every stone after that must be generated by the previous one (wood feeds fire, fire makes earth, earth bears metal, metal carries water, water nourishes wood). Get the order wrong and the stone shatters and the flood surges. The low ground floods first, so where you start and which path you take is the real decision in each run.

When all five are placed, the sky closes, the water recedes and the first people walk into the valley. The world is Nüwa's gift to them.

## 主题契合 / Theme
- 礼物不是写在字幕里：玩家每一次正确的炼石，都直接把一片世界"还"回来；结局把完整的世界交给人间。
- The gift is the mechanic: every correct stone returns a piece of the world, and the ending hands the whole world to the people who will live in it.

## 工具贡献 / Tool contribution
- World Labs Marble：生成整片山谷世界（Gaussian splat，SPZ）与碰撞网格；游戏在浏览器中用 Spark 渲染，并用自定义着色修改器按区域把世界从灰恢复成彩色。碰撞网格用于行走与地面高度。
- Tripo：生成五块五行原石（青玉、赤晶、黄玉、白石英、黑曜石）与三足铜鼎炉（GLB），作为玩家用手抓取、投掷、炼化的交互物件。
- World Labs Marble 世界链接：https://marble.worldlabs.ai/world/4ad1032d-1fae-45ca-ba24-3fc35b844eb4
- 协同：Marble 负责"世界"，Tripo 负责"改变世界的东西"。每放对一块 Tripo 石头，Marble 世界的一部分就恢复颜色。
- 运行时没有实时调用生成接口，所有资产在制作阶段生成并固定保存。

## 五音彩蛋 / Sound
每块石头补天时奏出对应的五音：木-角、火-徵、土-宫、金-商、水-羽。结局五音齐鸣。
